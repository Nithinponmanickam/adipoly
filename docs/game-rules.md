# ADIPOLY — Game Mechanics & Rules

## Core Concept

ADIPOLY is an original property negotiation and corporate investment board game. Players navigate a 32-space board, acquire technology sectors, manage liquidity through mortgages, trigger public auctions, and survive chaotic market events.

## Board Topology (Original 32-Space Layout)

The board consists of 32 distinct data-driven spaces:
- **Genesis Square (Space 0 - START)**: Collect ₹200 passing/landing dividend.
- **Incubation Hub (Spaces 1, 3)**: Low-cost early tech corridors (Startup Alley, Founder's Row).
- **Tax Audit Bureau (Space 4 - SPECIAL)**: Regulatory assessment: pay 10% cash holding.
- **Logistics Corridor (Spaces 5, 13, 21, 28)**: Interconnected high-speed transit (Hyperloop, Aero Cargo, Orbital Spaceport, Deep Sea Cable).
- **Computing Hub (Spaces 6, 7)**: Silicon Strip & Cyber Vault.
- **Regulatory Detention (Space 8 - JAIL)**: Detention cell. Entering grants an immediate 10% bail dividend.
- **BioTech Corridor (Spaces 9, 11)**: Biotech Lab & Nano Synthesis.
- **Exchange Floor (Space 12 - AUCTION)**: Central property bidding arena.
- **Clean Energy (Spaces 14, 15)**: Solar Grid & Fusion Station.
- **Venture Dividend (Space 16 - BONUS)**: Cash payout of 5% liquidity.
- **Cloud Tech (Spaces 17, 19)**: Cloud Corridor & Quantum Datacenter.
- **Gov Innovation Grant (Space 20 - BONUS)**: R&D grant of ₹150.
- **Media Syndicate (Spaces 22, 23)**: Media Syndicate & Broadcast Tower.
- **Regulatory Raid (Space 24 - SPECIAL)**: Forced detention: proceed directly to Detention.
- **Artificial Intelligence (Spaces 25, 26)**: AI Foundry & Neural Grid.
- **Apex Capital (Spaces 29, 31)**: Ultra-premium valuation nodes (Sovereign Wealth & Apex Tower).
- **High Risk Hedge (Space 30 - SPECIAL)**: 1-die roll wager (1-3 lose ₹100, 4-6 win ₹250).

## Unique Mechanics

### 1. Mortgages with Auto-Expiration
- Players can mortgage properties for instant cash.
- Unlike traditional board games, mortgages in ADIPOLY have an active grace period (default: 5 turns).
- If an unredeemed mortgage expires, the property is automatically seized and placed on the public **Exchange Floor Auction**!

### 2. Live Public Auctions
- When a landing player chooses to skip purchasing a property, or when a mortgage expires, an auction begins.
- All non-bankrupt players can place bids in real time.

### 3. Trade Fairness Analyzer
- Players can bundle cash, properties, and assets in bilateral trades.
- The built-in advisory engine computes an equity score (0-100) and highlights advantages/disadvantages (e.g. completes set, creates monopoly risk, excessive cash transfer).
- The analyzer is purely advisory: players are free to negotiate asymmetrical trades.

### 4. Regulatory Detention (Jail) Dividend
- In ADIPOLY, entering Detention grants the player an immediate 10% cash dividend on current reserves.
- Movement is restricted until escaping via doubles, paying bail fee, or playing an event card.

### 5. Chaos Mode
- 13 selectable modular modifiers:
  - `DOUBLE_RENT`: Global rent doubled.
  - `REVERSE_DIRECTION`: Counter-clockwise movement.
  - `VOLATILE_REAL_ESTATE`: Property values fluctuate each round.
  - `RENT_SPIKE`: Surprise multipliers on unmortgaged properties.
  - `BANK_BONUS`: Boosted dividends on bonus spaces.
  - `QUANTUM_WARP`: Teleportation nodes on specific spaces.
  - `SECTOR_BOOM`: Targeted valuation spikes.
  - `MARKET_CRASH`: 50% rental discount on random sectors.
  - `JACKPOT_DICE`: Cash bonuses on rolling doubles.
  - `FORCED_AUCTIONS`: All unowned landings trigger mandatory auctions.
  - `BAILOUT_FRENZY`: Enhanced detention rewards.
  - `UNDERDOG_RELIEF`: Subsidies for low-cash players.
  - `WEALTH_TAX`: Targeted audits for high-net-worth players.
